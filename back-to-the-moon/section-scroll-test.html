Prologue

July 14, 1964

The Artemis spacesuits, designed for lunar exploration, represent a significant advancement over the Apollo suits. They offer enhanced mobility, improved safety features, and are designed for a wider range of tasks and environmental conditions. The Artemis suits are also designed for longer missions and are more customizable than their Apollo-era predecessors.

Compare the Apollo and Artemis spacesuit designs.

    Mobility:

    Artemis suits (AxEMU):

  • Feature more joints and a greater range of motion, allowing for more complex tasks like squatting and bending.

    • Apollo suits:

  • Were more restrictive, limiting movement and requiring more effort for certain actions.

    • Safety and Durability:

    Artemis suits:

    . Incorporate advanced materials and designs to protect against hazards like micrometeoroids and extreme temperatures on the Moon.

    Apollo suits:

    While effective, they were not designed for the same level of durability and protection against the specific lunar environment.

    Lunar dust:

    Artemis suits are designed with features to mitigate the effects of lunar dust, which is known to be a problem on the Moon.

    Joints: . The Apollo suits had vulnerable soft joints, while the Artemis suits use more robust hard shell components for better longevity.

    Mission Capabilities:

    Artemis suits:

  • Are designed for longer missions and more complex tasks than Apollo, enabling more extensive scientific exploration.

    • Apollo suits:

  • Were designed for shorter missions and focused primarily on landing and returning. Other Notable Differences:

    • Entry:

    Artemis suits have a rear-entry design, like a butterfly emerging from a chrysalis, while Apollo suits had a different entry system, according to a report by SYFY.

    Cost:

    Artemis suits are significantly more expensive, reflecting the advanced technology and capabilities.

    Color:

    The prototype Artemis suit has a distinctive black and blue/orange color scheme, while the final production suits are expected to be white for better thermal control, unlike the classic Apollo suits.
